Let me tell you about thirteen seconds that changed music forever.

Las Vegas. September 7, 1996. Quarter past eleven at night.

A black BMW stops at a red light. Suge Knight is driving. Tupac Shakur sits beside him.

Now look to your right.

A white Cadillac pulls up. Slowly.

The back window comes down.

Tupac is hit four times. Six days later, he is dead.

But here's what nobody tells you. It started hours earlier… in a hotel lobby.
