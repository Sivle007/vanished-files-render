Twenty-nine years after that red light, a jury finally heard the story.

Las Vegas, August 2026. No gun. No fingerprints. No new witness.

Just his own words. Every interview. Every page of his book.

The defence called it street stories, told for money.

The jury needed less than three hours.

Guilty. Forty years to life. He says he'll appeal.

But one question never reached a courtroom. According to him, the shooter was his own nephew… who died in 1998.

The case is closed. The questions aren't.
